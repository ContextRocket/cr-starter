# Source-site fidelity policy

For a website port, the captured source is the content and route authority.
The goal is to make the existing site easier to maintain and improve its
presentation without silently turning it into a different marketing site.

## Copy and claims

- Preserve source wording, names, numbers, testimonials, and their attributions
  verbatim by default. Keep the source URL or capture reference with imported
  content so reviewers can check it.
- Do not invent or duplicate calls to action, benefits, claims, testimonials,
  customer relationships, or product capabilities. A CTA belongs only where
  the source has one, and its label and destination should match the source.
- Do not silently rewrite copy for tone, grammar, or SEO. Record a proposed
  correction with its source evidence and get the site owner's approval before
  changing published copy. Platform-required labels and consent text should be
  clearly separated from the customer's authored content.
- Keep layout-only changes out of content files. A content diff should be
  reviewable independently from a visual or component change.

## Design and structure

- Preserve the source page's section order, visual identity, assets, public
  routes, and information density unless a specific change is recorded as a
  proposal.
- Improvements may address responsive behavior, accessibility, loading,
  alignment, spacing, and asset efficiency. They should not add sections or
  reshape the hierarchy in ways that change the site's message.
- Align navigation and page content to a shared responsive grid. Check the
  header, hero, section headings, copy columns, proof, testimonials, and forms
  together at desktop and mobile widths.
- Use the original page as the visual comparison. A successful build or a
  polished isolated component does not demonstrate source fidelity.

## Review record

Before editing a port, record its source URL, route inventory, section order,
copy and claim sources, and representative screenshots. After editing, review
the copy diff separately from the visual diff. List intentional changes and
their evidence in the fork's gap analysis. If a proposed change lacks evidence
or owner approval, keep the source version.
